The challenge

Higher education opens doors. Navigating it isn't always easy.

Between academic expectations, personal responsibilities, finding community, and accessing support, students can face a complicated journey. We believe there is an opportunity to make that journey more connected and manageable.

01

Finding belonging

Meeting people and building meaningful relationships can be difficult, especially in unfamiliar environments.

02

Finding support

Helpful services and opportunities may exist, but students don't always know where to look or what is relevant to them.

03

Finding momentum

Balancing academic goals with everyday life requires planning, encouragement, and practical tools.

Our social mission

No student should have to navigate their journey alone.

Common Room is an early-stage, social-impact EdTech venture with a simple ambition: to help students feel connected, supported, and equipped to thrive.

We're exploring an institution-funded model designed to make the core student experience available without individual subscription fees.

We believe success should be measured not only by how many students we reach, but by the meaningful difference we make in their lives.
